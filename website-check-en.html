The seventeen checks

  • Legal notice (Impressum) (EU)
  • Privacy policy (EU)
  • Fonts loaded from Google's servers (EU)
  • Tracking without visible consent (EU)
  • Tap-to-call phone number
  • Email address findable
  • Opening hours or address
  • Page title
  • Meta description
  • H1 heading
  • Structured data (Schema.org)
  • Link preview (Open Graph)
  • HTTPS
  • No insecure embeds
  • Mobile rendering
  • Response time
  • Enquiries outside business hours

What this is not

Only the publicly reachable front page is checked: its HTML source, its response time, and the pages linked from it. Not checked: the CONTENTS of a legal notice or privacy policy, subpages, server configuration, and anything visible only after signing in. Four of the seventeen checks are European obligations and are marked as such. This is not legal advice.
