Where do opening hours and address belong on a website?

What it is about, what can honestly be measured about it from outside — and how to check it yourself without buying anything.

What it is about

Neither opening hours nor an address can be made out on the front page.

This one is not about any single country and its law. It holds wherever the site is.

What can actually be measured

Whether hours or a street address appear on the front page itself, rather than only in the legal notice.

That is deliberately narrow: only what is visible from outside, in the source of the front page. Nothing else appears in the finding either — anyone claiming more is guessing.

What to do

Put hours and address visibly on the front page, not only in the legal notice.
